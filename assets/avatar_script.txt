In December 2009, a film arrived that would shatter every box office record ever set. James Cameron had spent over a decade building a world no one had ever seen.

Pandora is a moon orbiting a gas giant, covered in glowing forests and floating mountains. The Na'vi live in harmony with Eywa, a living planet that connects every tree, creature, and dream.

Humans arrive in search of unobtanium, a mineral worth a fortune - and everything begins to burn. Jake Sully is a paraplegic Marine who arrives to replace his dead brother, expecting nothing more than a simple job.

Instead, Jake bonds with the Na'vi, falls in love with Neytiri, and his legs work again on Pandora. When the corporation destroys the Hometree - the sacred home of the Na'vi - Jake is forced to choose a side.

The final battle is pure cinema: AMP suits, thunderbirds, and Eywa herself fighting back. The film's last line became legendary - arguably the most powerful moment in modern blockbuster history.

Avatar became the highest-grossing film of all time, a record it held for over a decade. Cameron didn't just make a movie - he built a universe.

Thirteen years later, Avatar: Fire and Ash proved Pandora was never finished with us. And in 2029, the third film arrives - one of the most anticipated releases in cinema history.
